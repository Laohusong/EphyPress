# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Initial independent EphyPress distribution of Level1 p53 lossless compression.
- Bundled, pinned BSD FSE implementation; build-time C++ extension and ABI3 wheels.
- Atomic compression/decompression, full source verification and bounded readers.
- Compatible decoding of existing lossless p53/p41 SPC1/SPC3 archives.
- Direct native KS4 helper and public `file_object` integration.
- Serializable SpikeInterface extractor and streaming writer with channel/probe metadata.
- English and Chinese guides, CPU integration tests, CI and release packaging.
